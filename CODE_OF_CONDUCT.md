# Code of Conduct

labdog follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory behaviour are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately using GitHub's "Report content" option on the offending comment or issue, or by contacting an organization owner through GitHub. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.
